Company Profile

Reliance Finance Limited

Reliance Finance Limited is a national-level "C" class financial institution, licensed by Nepal Rastra Bank. Since our establishment in 2066 B.S., we have positioned ourselves as a key driver of retail banking, agricultural credit, and MSME growth inside and outside the Kathmandu Valley.

We pride ourselves on sound corporate governance, risk management, and capital safety. Our promotions represent pioneers across various commercial ventures, trading, and infrastructure domains, ensuring that we act not just as a financial lender, but as a long-term strategic growth partner.

With our registered head office in Kamaladi, Kathmandu, we manage 21 branches spread in key commercial hubs of Nepal, delivering customized savings, remittance, digital wallets, and loan services.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Quick guides regarding account operations and loan enquiries.

Q: How can I register for mobile banking?

A: You can apply online using the RFL Smart App or visit your local branch to fill out a registration form. A temporary activation pin will be sent to your mobile.

Q: What is the maximum tenure for a Home Loan?

A: RFL provides flexible repayment tenures for Home Loans up to 20 years, depending on valuation structures and loan compliance.

Q: Can I take a loan against my Fixed Deposit?

A: Yes, you can borrow up to 90% of your Fixed Deposit amount. Interest charged is typically +2.0% above your FD rate, and processing is done within a few hours.

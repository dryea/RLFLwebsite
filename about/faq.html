Frequently Asked Questions

Quick guides regarding account operations and loan enquiries:

Q: How can I register for mobile banking?

A: You can apply online using the RFL Smart App or visit your local branch to fill out a registration form. A temporary activation pin will be sent to your mobile.

Q: What is the maximum tenure for a Home Loan?

A: RFL provides flexible repayment tenures for Home Loans up to 20 years, depending on valuation structures and loan compliance.

Q: Can I take a loan against my Fixed Deposit?

A: Yes, you can borrow up to 90% of your Fixed Deposit amount. Interest charged is typically +2.0% above your FD rate, and processing is done within a few hours.

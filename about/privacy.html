Privacy Policy

Reliance Finance Limited is committed to protecting the privacy and safety of information shared by depositors, loan applicants, and mobile banking users. Customer accounts information, transaction records, and contact details are stored under high-grade network firewalls and are never disclosed to third-parties except under legal warrants from regulating authorities.

We utilize administrative and technical safeguard mechanisms conforming with regulatory requirements. Your dynamic personal coordinates (like mobile number, email coordinates, IP locations, and device IDs) are used solely to offer secure services, prevent hacking logs, and activate requested portals.

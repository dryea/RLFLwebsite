Remittance Fixed Deposit (NRN)

Dedicated investment scheme for Non-Resident Nepalese (NRN) and migrant workers abroad. RFL rewards foreign savings with an additional interest premium (+1.0%).

View All Fixed Deposits Products & Services / Fixed Deposits / Remittance Fixed Deposit (NRN)

Key Features & Benefits

Guaranteed +1.0% interest premium above standard individual FD rates
Remote account opening and paperless document collection
Interest payout directly credited to savings or repatriated
Priority online banking and support desk coordination

Required Documents

To invest in this Fixed Deposit, please prepare the following original documents along with their photocopies:

Citizenship Certificate copy
Valid Passport copy with foreign work permit / student visa copy
Non-Resident Nepalese (NRN) Card copy (if applicable)
Two copies of passport-size photos
Proof of inward remittance (Swift copy / Remittance receipt)

Investment Overview

Minimum Deposit NPR 50,000.00
Interest Rates 4.50% to 7.25% p.a.
Compounding Payouts Quarterly / Maturity

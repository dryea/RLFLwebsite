Personal Loan

Key Features & Benefits

Hassle-free loan processing with minimum audit checks
Flexible repayment schedules up to 10 years
No strict usage restrictions (travel, emergency, health, marriage)
Simple documentation and fast valuation clearance

Required Documents

To apply for this, please prepare the following original documents along with their photocopies:

Citizenship copy and PP photos of applicant and guarantor
Land/House ownership certificate (Lalpurja)
Blue print and Naksa of property
Char Killa (boundary certificate) from ward office
Income source document (salary certificate or rental contract)
Tax clearance certificate of property
